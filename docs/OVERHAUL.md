# Plant overhaul

Goal: a pressurized-water plant the player builds, wires and operates: moving rods, bound controls and instruments, a real cooling tower, a turbine that drives Create machinery, and accidents that grow out of the simulated state.

## What stays

- **Vessel:** casing shell with fuel, control and coolant columns in any valid size, the controller, ports, per-block diagnostics and casing integrity.
- **Process state:** `CoolantBuffer` carries fluid, heat, phase and pressure. Splits and merges conserve mass and heat. Create transfers keep heat between coolant machines.
- **Circuits:** the Circulation Pump traces a single-path loop and moves coolant through every tank and pipe on it. Pipes hold coolant, and hot coolant bursts ordinary Create pipes.
- **Fuel lifecycle, radiation, contamination and shielding.**

## What changes

| Area | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Turbine | Fixed 16 RPM, takes 2% of steam energy every tick whatever the load | Rated speed set on the turbine. A governor valve follows the shaft load, with rotor overspeed and an overspeed trip on sudden load loss |
| Electricity | None | The plant's own electrical system, in its own units and without Forge Energy: Generators on the turbine shaft, wires strung between terminals, breakers, switches, transformers, buses, Electric Motors, standby power and a Grid Connection that buys and sells. The turbine shaft can still drive Create machinery directly |
| Control rods | Drive holds a number | Visible rod assembly that travels with the real insertion, rod banks, bank controls |
| Inspection | Goggles only | Vessel viewport block; rods and fuel glow visible through it |
| Controls | Redstone on the controller and drives | The Reactor Alloy Dashboard with buttons, switches, levers, gauges and alarms bound straight to plant machines or to Redstone Links, each with a settings screen. Reactor Signal Interfaces do the same from redstone pulses |
| Primary pressure | None (liquid coolant has no pressure) | Pressurizer on the primary loop: heaters, spray, relief; low pressure lets coolant boil in the core |
| Cooling tower | Stack of packing blocks | Built tower: fill standing on its outlet pipe, the hot inlet in the side of the top block, make-up water piped into the fill, a Create Encased Fan on top, driven through the fill's own shaft; stacked fill joins into one glazed column that shows its water level; capacity from fill, water and airflow; makeup water and evaporation |
| Feedwater | Loop pump only | A Create Fluid Tank inline in the loop with a make-up pump on a Threshold Switch, a feed pump that cavitates on steam, and a Heat Exchanger as feedwater heater |
| Reactivity | Rods and fuel only | Fuel-temperature and coolant-temperature feedback; operating modes with start-up permissives |
| Accidents | Trip, cracks, breach leak | Fuel damage, clad failure, core melt, corium, burn-through, wreckage and contaminated blocks that persist |
| Guidance | README | Ponder scenes, reference plant function |

## Phases (dependency order)

1. **A. Energy chain.** Turbine governor and overspeed. The Generator built in this phase was later removed. The electrical system that replaced it is described under Electricity in the README: generators, wires, breakers and a grid connection, in the mod's own units.
2. **B. Rods and inspection.** Visible rod travel, banks, viewport.
3. **C. Controls and instruments.** Dashboard and parts, part settings, Signal Interface jobs, alarm signal.
4. **D. Plant equipment.** Pressurizer, feedwater tank, built cooling tower with fans.
5. **E. Reactor behaviour.** Temperature feedback, operating modes, permissives, protective trips through installed equipment.
6. **F. Accidents.** Progressive core damage, corium, persistent wreckage, config limits.
7. **G. Guidance.** Ponder scenes, reference plant, end-to-end scenario tests.

## Status

- **A to F:** on main, each with game tests and an in-game check.
- **G:** the operating manual is `docs/OPERATING.md`, the reference plant is generated by `src/inspect/scenes/make_facility.py`, and `plantStartsRunsGeneratesAndShutsDown` is the end-to-end scenario test.
- **Control room:** a linked wall of Plant Screens shows the reactor display with trends and events, Display Link sources feed smaller walls and Create displays, and the controls are Reactor Alloy Dashboards with parts bound to the plant. `src/inspect/scenes/make_facility_look.py` builds the reference plant, runs it and photographs every room.
- **Create-first pass:** the mod's own tanks, tower distributor, tower fan, fuel bench, alarm siren, generator and breaker panel are gone. Loops run through Create's pipes and Fluid Tanks, hot runs are reinforced in place with a Reactor Alloy Ingot, the turbine shaft is the output, screens run on rotation and the Pressurizer on a Blaze Burner. A Centrifuge adds enrichment to the fuel chain.
- **Electricity:** on main, built after the Create-first pass and described under Electricity in the README.
  - **Equipment:** Generators, Power Wire, Power Cable and Transmission Line, Circuit Breakers with protection relays, Disconnect and Earthing Switches, Transformers with tap changers, Bus Bars, Electric Motors, Insulators, a Surge Arrester and a Grid Connection.
  - **Standby power:** a Station Battery, a Diesel Engine and a Transfer Switch.
  - **Behaviour:** synchronising, frequency and voltage that move with load, short circuits, overloads, lightning, rain and grid disturbances in storms, wear, and wrecks that are repaired where they stand.
  - **Control room:** a Plant Screen wall linked to a Bus Bar draws the network as a one-line diagram, and Dashboard Alarm lamps watch electrical equipment.
- **Ponder:** thirty-four scenes, built by `src/inspect/scenes/make_ponder.py`. The reactor plant has the vessel, control rods, working the rods from the desk with the rod lever and Power Regulator, the primary loop, the steam loop, the feedwater train, rejecting heat, refuelling, borating and diluting the coolant, refuelling one column at a time through Fuel Channel Caps with the core map, the Area Radiation Monitor, cleaning the coolant with a Demineralizer, Heat Exchanger tube leaks, growing Chartrine from Contaminated Coolant, pump seals, taking a pipe through a containment wall, and holding coolant in a Fluid Tank. Nuclear fuel has enrichment, making a Fuel Assembly, reprocessing and sealing waste in canisters. The control room has the control desk and the Plant Screens. The electrics have wires, the Circuit Breaker, synchronising, standby power and repair. Accidents have the protection bypass, meltdown, fallout, the Water Cannon, the Meltdown Siren and recovery after an explosion.
- **Recovery:** a nuclear explosion leaves work behind it: Core Debris thrown against whatever survived, Wreckage where the plant stood, contamination on the shell that stopped the blast, and a recorded site that reads all clear when every part of it is back under 1 mSv/min. Seven advancements follow the work, the last for Charred Planks.
- **Advancements:** thirty-six in one tree, from digging up uranium through fuel, the reactor, the grid, the control room and radiation to the accidents and the recovery after them.
- **Boron:** the primary coolant carries boron the way it carries heat, through every split and merge. Borax comes from crushed Scoria and is mixed into Boric Acid, which a Create pump puts into the vessel through a spare port. Clean coolant dilutes it, letdown takes its share out, a leak loses it and boiling leaves it behind. The reference station has no boron line yet.
- **Refuelling by column:** a Fuel Channel Cap in the vessel's top face gives the fuel column under it its own way in and out, for hands, funnels, Mechanical Arms and Deployers, so part-burnt assemblies can be shuffled between columns. A Plant Screen wall linked to a cap or the Service Port draws the core from above as a map of what each column has left, its temperature, the rods and the caps. The reference station does not use caps or a core map.
- **Pipes:** a Wrench locks a Reinforced Pipe straight so it joins nothing beside its run, and Shielding Concrete encases a straight one into a full block that shields and stops the blast, for passing through a containment wall.
- **Pump seals:** a Circulation Pump's seals wear while it runs, faster while it cavitates, and from three quarters worn they leak coolant out at the pump. Primary coolant contaminates the floor there, which an Area Radiation Monitor in the pump room reads. Sealing Gaskets renew the seals with the pump stopped.
- **Tube leaks:** a Heat Exchanger's tubes wear with the heat they pass, and from three quarters worn they leak primary coolant into the steam side. The leak carries activity round the steam loop, where it gives a dose and halves every minute, so an Area Radiation Monitor in the turbine hall catches it. Reactor Alloy Sheets plug the tubes with the exchanger stopped and cold. The reference station has not been run long enough to wear its tubes.
- **Tanks keep the figure:** a Create Fluid Tank keeps the boron, heat and contamination share of the coolant or Boric Acid in it, through any pipe or pump, so a holding tank or a borated make-up tank works. The Plant Linker remembers a tank for a gauge, a Plant Screen wall and a Display Link.
- **Coolant clean-up:** coolant carries its contamination the way it carries heat and boron, a share that splits and merges with it, and reads as Contaminated Coolant over one part in a hundred. A Demineralizer on a pumped side line takes the contamination out into a Resin Cartridge, which is then radioactive waste for a Shielded Cask. The share shows as the Coolant activity reading on the reactor display, gauges and Display Links. The reference station has no clean-up line.
- **Chartrine:** a uranium-bearing stone in four shades with Create's full set of cut blocks, in a Building section of the creative tab. It lies in layered Overworld veins made by Create's layered ore feature and crushes into Crushed Raw Uranium 12 times in 100. It is renewable: Contaminated Coolant leaving an open Fluid Pipe end over lava turns the lava to stone, a block for about every 250 mB, and the chance of Chartrine against tuff is the share of the coolant's contamination on the reactor's record.
- **The reactor's record:** coolant carries, beside its contamination, how much of it a failed core made. It splits, merges and decays with the coolant and is lost in any store that keeps no record, a bucket among them, where the coolant still counts as fully contaminated. It reads as the Reactor's share of activity on a gauge, a Plant Screen wall and a Display Link, from the Reactor Controller and a Fluid Tank. Reprocessing waste has no record.
- **Open pipe ends:** Contaminated Coolant that leaves an open Create pipe end with no lava under it dirties the floor as a leak of it does.
- **Buckets:** the four liquids have buckets that fill and empty through Create's Spout and Item Drain. Emptied on the ground a bucket spills and is lost, and coolant dirties the spot. A dispenser pours one into a tank it faces and spills it otherwise. Steam has none.
- **Charred wood:** the Charred Log strips with an axe and makes planks, stairs, a slab, a fence, a gate, a door, a trapdoor, a pressure plate, a button, a sign and a hanging sign. The saw does what Create does for any wood.
- **Area monitors:** an Area Radiation Monitor hangs on a wall and reads the dose rate there. It ticks with the rate, and at the alarm level set on its value box it lights, sounds and gives a redstone signal. The Plant Linker remembers it, so a Plant Screen wall, a Dashboard Gauge, a Dashboard Alarm lamp and a Display Link can show a hall's radiation in the control room. The reference station has none.
- **Reference station:** each steam loop has a feedwater heater and a make-up pump on a Threshold Switch, the rod drives are in banks A, B and D, the reactor desk has two rod levers and raise, lower and a setpoint gauge for the Power Regulator, a reprocessing hall on its own feeder stands south of the fuel building, with a canning bench that fills, gaskets and presses waste canisters from its liquid waste tank, and two Meltdown Sirens linked to the reactor hang in the control room and on the service building's front wall. Its water comes from the world: each tower's make-up pump draws from the basin under the tower, a well pump in the turbine hall basement fills the two make-up tanks, and one in the hot cell fills the reprocessing hall's water tank.
- **Not done:** The reprocessing gallery was measured with four cooled Spent Fuel Assemblies on the line: it reads 0.03 mSv/min with the line empty, from the waste vault next door; up to 0.5 at the feed port while fuel is on the first saw; up to 1.1 in front of the product port while the pieces are in the dissolver; and under 0.2 everywhere else behind the wall while the fuel is in work. Both ports are open holes in the wall. The four assemblies leave 1,600 mB of Contaminated Coolant, and the full Spout on the canning bench then reads about 1.1 to someone working at it. The waste tank stands in the wall line, and full, at 24,000 mB, it read 15 in front of it, so a Shielding Concrete pier now stands on the gallery side of it and the same tank reads 0.5.

Each phase lands with game tests and an in-game check before the next starts. Saves: existing blocks keep their ids and data; new state defaults to the old behaviour when absent.

## Units

- Heat: HU. Water: 1 HU per mB per °C, boils at 80 HU/mB above ambient, 540 HU/mB latent.
- Shaft power: SU. 1 HU/t of steam work is 4 SU.
